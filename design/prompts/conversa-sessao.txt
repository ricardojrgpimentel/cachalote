Cachalote is an Android app (Material 3, dark-first, portrait phone) that runs an AI coding agent
locally on the user's phone. Design the OPEN CONVERSATION SCREEN — what the user reaches after
tapping one of the rows of the conversation list. It is a focused, full-screen chat, exactly like
WhatsApp's chat view: the BOTTOM NAVIGATION BAR IS GONE. The composer is the only thing at the
bottom edge of the screen.

Top app bar: a back arrow on the left, then the conversation title in bold, one line, truncated
with an ellipsis (real example: "corre o comando echo cachalote-teste"); underneath the title a
compact monospace status line "ROOT · deepseek-v4-flash · contexto 6 290 / 1 000 000 · 1%" with a
small green dot. On the right two icon actions: a "Fechar a sessão" glyph and an overflow menu with
"Modelo", "Esforço de raciocínio" and "Fechar a sessão activa".

Transcript, scrollable, with generous vertical spacing:
- user messages: rounded bubbles aligned RIGHT, raised surface, at most 80% of the width, with a
  small monospace timestamp underneath;
- agent messages: LEFT aligned plain text, no bubble, generous line height;
- reasoning blocks: muted italic text with a small sparkle glyph and a chevron to collapse them;
- tool-call cards: compact rounded cards with a leading glyph, the tool name in monospace
  (examples: android_see, android_vscreen, bash, android_tap), a status chip — "a correr" with a
  small spinner, "concluída", "falhada" — and a one-line argument preview;
- system notices: small centred pill chips (examples: "sessão 33c12f8c criada", "a acessibilidade
  voltou a estar ligada");
- errors: a discreet card with a red outline, a small warning glyph and a "Tentar novamente" text
  button.

Above the composer, only when it has tasks in it, a compact task-queue strip: "Fila de tarefas",
"2 na fila", small pill buttons "Juntar à fila" and "Correr a fila".

Composer pinned to the very bottom edge, with NO navigation bar below it (this is the point):
one wide rounded text field with the placeholder "Escreve uma tarefa para o agente", a small
paperclip glyph INSIDE the field on the left and a microphone glyph INSIDE the field on the right,
plus a round filled send button outside on the right; while the agent is working that round button
becomes a stop button. The composer respects the phone's bottom gesture inset.

Style: calm, technical, trustworthy; deep blue-grey dark surfaces with a teal/cyan accent; 16dp
rounded corners; strong typographic hierarchy; monospace for technical strings and timestamps;
generous 48dp touch targets; no gradients. All interface labels in European Portuguese.
