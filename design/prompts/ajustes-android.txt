Cachalote is an Android app (Material 3, dark-first, portrait phone) that runs an AI coding agent
locally on the user's phone. Design the SETTINGS SCREEN. The important part: the list rows must
look like the **Android system Settings app** (the "Definições" app on a Pixel) — tall, comfortable
rows with a leading icon in a tonal circle, a title with a supporting line underneath, a thin
chevron and a divider. They must NOT look like thin text lines.

Top: the title "Ajustes" with a compact line underneath, "8 módulos configurados". The app's own
header stays above it (brand mark, "motor DSH no telefone · ligado" with a green dot, and the ROOT
chip).

The list group is a rounded surface (16dp corners, 1dp outline) holding eight rows. Every row:
- is 72dp tall and spans the full width;
- starts with the section glyph, 22dp, centred inside a 40dp circle with a tonal container
  (a slightly lighter surface than the group, no outline);
- then a title in 16sp semibold, and directly underneath a supporting line in 14sp, muted;
- ends with a thin 24dp chevron at the far right, not a big text arrow;
- has a 1dp divider after it, inset so that it starts where the row's text starts (it never passes
  under the circle);
- the whole row (not just the text) is the touch target and ripples when pressed.

The eight rows, with their supporting lines:
1. "Motor" — "estado, modos (root, Shizuku, embutido) e diagnóstico"
2. "Chave do provedor" — "a chave do provedor, guardada no daemon"
3. "Capacidades autorizadas" — "o que o agente pode fazer no telefone"
4. "Acessibilidade" — "ver e tocar no ecrã"
5. "Áreas permitidas do bash" — "onde o agente corre sem pedir autorização"
6. "Servidores MCP" — "ferramentas externas ligadas por JSON"
7. "Ecrã virtual" — "um segundo ecrã onde o agente trabalha"
8. "Backup e licenças" — "exportar definições e ver o software de terceiros"

Below the group, with a normal gap, one more full-height row in its own rounded surface with a thin
accent outline: "Repetir a configuração inicial" with the supporting line "Voltar a correr o
assistente de boas-vindas" and the same tonal circle with a restart glyph.

Below that, a warning card with a thin red outline: a small triangle glyph, the micro-label
"PERIGO", and three lines of text: "Aviso: o motor corre como root e o Android não impõe o sandbox
do DSH. O que trava um comando fora do workspace é a autorização que aprovas aqui — e tudo fica
registado."

Bottom navigation with three items: Conversa, Resumo, Ajustes (active).

Style: calm, technical, trustworthy; deep blue-grey dark surfaces with a teal/cyan accent; strong
typographic hierarchy; generous 48dp+ touch targets; no gradients; the rows must feel like the
Android Settings app, only in this app's colours. All interface labels in European Portuguese.
